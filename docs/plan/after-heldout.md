# After the held-out run: backlog of reviewer improvements (Planner A, 2026-11-11)

**Nothing here runs before the tag `review-freeze-3` and the end of H2.** No item is approved, budgeted or scheduled.
It is a list for Sunny to choose from once the result is in. Companion to [`plan-v2`](2026-11-11-plan-v2.md).

## Rules that apply to every item
1. **The held-out set is spent once it is scored.** After H2 the 29 cases may be read and become a second dev set. A
   claim that a change helps then needs cases nobody tuned on (item T1), or it is reported as "dev only".
2. **Money.** What is left after the run is the reserve ($3.00), what remains of the unallocated $1.18 after decision
   077, and whatever the capped jobs did not use (`docs/budget.md`). Every cost below is an **estimate** from the unit
   costs measured on non-TypeScript PRs (`docs/budget.md` "Unit costs") until R1's TypeScript figures replace them.
3. **One pre-registration, one reviewer, one run** per item, with the gate fixed before the run and its error range
   stated, as in decision 076. The 3-case margin on 31 dev bugs has a false-reject rate of 14–80% (`run/out/bunny.plan-audit.md`
   §5): a "FAIL" on dev is weak evidence, and so is a "PASS".
4. **An idea that already lost comes back only with a reason it would turn out differently** (056, 058, 064, 065, 070,
   071, 073, 074, 075, levers v2). Items below say where they touch a lost lever.

## Not worth doing (so nobody proposes them again without new evidence)
- A cheaper verifier model: the verifier is already about an eighth of the cost (`bunny.plan-audit.md` 7f); the gain is
  under a cent per PR.
- Another judge model or a third judge: the hand-check, not the judge, decides the held-out result.
- Re-tuning on the old 40-PR dev set, Cal.com or ReviewBench: none is TypeScript, and Cal.com is spent.
- Threshold, vote, evidence-verifier, recall-lens, Ultra, split-halves and schema-retry variants (056, 064, 065, 070,
  071, 074, 075): each lost under a pre-registered gate, and nothing since gives a reason to expect otherwise.
