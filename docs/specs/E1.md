**Caps procedures (decision 076c item 1; Planner B's D1 note §1.3; `docs/budget.md` "076c … caps procedures"):**

## 2. The stop/go rule (decision 076g item 6; recorded before E1; read once, by artu1, after codex's check)
On the 31 dev bugs, between A0 and CodeRabbit:
- **GO** if A0's catches ≥ CodeRabbit's − 3 **and** A0's comments per PR ≤ 2 × CodeRabbit's;
- **RETHINK** if CodeRabbit leads by 6 or more;
- anything in between is **INCONCLUSIVE** and waits for held-out.

With d = C(CodeRabbit) − C(A0):

| d | A0's comments per PR ≤ 2 × CodeRabbit's | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 3 or less | yes | GO |
| 3 or less | no | INCONCLUSIVE |
| 4 or 5 | either | INCONCLUSIVE |
| 6 or more | either | RETHINK |

**How the rule's words are read. The ruling does not fix these four points; they are fixed here, before any number
exists, and are not changed afterwards:**
1. **The judge is bench-judge-v1** (the scorer of record, decision 060). The same table read on x1's catches is
   reported beside it and changes nothing.
2. **Catches** are C from `l1.summary.json`. A dev bug whose PR CodeRabbit did not review is not caught by CodeRabbit.
3. **Comments per PR** is M: mean counted comments per case, for both rows over the dev cases CodeRabbit completed.
4. **Coverage.** If CodeRabbit completed fewer than 30 of the 40 dev cases, the rule is not read: the verdict is
   INCONCLUSIVE (coverage), with the count.
Greptile is not in the rule. Its row is reported.

**What follows from the verdict.** GO and INCONCLUSIVE: F1 goes ahead; the window runs. **RETHINK: artu1 tells
Sunny the same day, with the table; the tag waits for his answer** (goals are Sunny's, 076g item 8). The verdict, d,
the two comment means and the completion count go into decision 077 and into `docs/bench/ts-dev.md`. The rule is not
re-argued once the numbers exist: no other judge, cap or subset is tried to move a verdict.

## 9. The three most likely ways to get this wrong
1. **Reading the stop/go rule generously, or twice.** A lead of 4 is INCONCLUSIVE, not GO. The rule is read on v1,
   once, after codex's check. A second look with the other judge, another cap or a subset is a new experiment that
   nobody ruled.
2. **Treating a report-only row as a result.** A1, A2 and the Claude rows adopt nothing and change nothing in the
   freeze. Only Sunny can register a Claude row for held-out, and only by 16 Nov.
3. **Judging before protocol revision 10 is on `main`, or beside another paid process.** The first makes the pooled
   analysis no longer pre-registered (076g item 5). The second breaks the $0.40 envelope: the Claude rows' reviews
   are Sunny's money, but their judging and every other call here is Nebius money under L1's line.
